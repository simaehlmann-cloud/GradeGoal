2. Welche Daten verarbeitet die App?

In der App eingegebene Angaben – Noten, Fächer, Wunschnoten, Ziele, Notizen sowie wahlweise Name und Schule – werden ausschließlich lokal im Speicher des Geräts abgelegt (localStorage der App-Ansicht). Sie werden zu keinem Zeitpunkt an den Entwickler oder an Dritte übermittelt.

Die App baut im laufenden Betrieb keine Netzwerkverbindung auf. Sie funktioniert vollständig offline; Schriftarten und Programmcode sind fest eingebaut.

2. What the app processes

Grades, subjects, wish grades, goals, notes and optionally your name and school are stored only in your device's local storage. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to third parties. The app makes no network requests during use and works fully offline.

3. No collection, no sharing

  • No account, no sign-in, no registration
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No sharing with third parties
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone

4. Diagnostic log

The app keeps a local log of technical messages to help with troubleshooting. It never leaves your device and can be viewed and cleared in the settings.

5. Backup files

“Export backup” creates a file containing your entries. Where you store or send it is entirely your choice; the developer has no access to it.

6. PIN lock

The optional PIN lock is a privacy screen, not encryption. Your data remains unencrypted in local storage. Only a salted hash of the PIN is stored, never the PIN itself.

7. Permissions

The app requests no special Android or iOS permissions.

8. Use by children

The app is also intended for students under 13. Because no personal data is collected, stored or transmitted, no parental consent is required.

9. Your rights

As the developer holds no data about you, requests for access, rectification or erasure under Art. 15 ff. GDPR have no subject matter. You can delete all data yourself at any time via “Reset everything” or by uninstalling the app.
