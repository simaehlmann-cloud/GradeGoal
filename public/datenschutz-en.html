Privacy policy

Applies to the app GradeGoal (Apple App Store and Google Play) and to the web version.

In short: GradeGoal collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no servers operated by the provider, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no sharing with third parties. The only connection the app makes on its own is asking the app store whether the unlock has been purchased (section 12).

2. Processing on your device only

What you enter in the app never leaves your device. The data is kept in the app's local storage (localStorage of the embedded view). In the app from the store, every state is additionally saved in the operating system's app preferences (iOS: UserDefaults, Android: SharedPreferences). This second copy also stays in the app's protected area on the device and only serves to prevent data loss if the system clears the view's storage.

Because this data never reaches the provider, the provider does not process any personal data. The following overview nevertheless describes what the app stores, so that you can see what you decide about.

3. Entering other people's data

The app is also aimed at parents and teachers. If you enter grades or names of other people – a child, or your students – you process personal data under your own responsibility. Such data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to the provider.

  • Grades are particularly sensitive. Teachers should observe their school's rules and the data protection law of their federal state; in several German states, processing student data on private devices is restricted or requires approval.
  • Using first names or initials only is advisable. Name and school fields are entirely optional; terms and profiles can be given neutral labels.
  • The report card, an exported chart image, a backup and a CSV table contain the names you entered.

The app does not evaluate anyone. It only calculates with the numbers you enter.

  • No account, no sign-in, no registration
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no tracking
  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting
  • No sharing with third parties, no processors
  • No transfers to third countries by the provider

Fonts and program code are built in; nothing is loaded from third-party servers.

5. Legal basis

Insofar as local storage constitutes processing at all, it rests on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – it is necessary to perform the contract for use of the app, because grades could not be managed without storing them. Storage on your device is also covered by § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG as strictly necessary to provide the service you explicitly requested; no consent banner is therefore required.

6. Retention

Data stays on the device until you delete it: individually in the app, entirely via “Reset everything” in the settings, or by uninstalling the app. The provider has no retention period because the provider stores nothing.

7. Backup, table and report card

“Export backup” creates a JSON file containing everything you entered. You can also export your grades as a CSV table and output the report card – in the browser via the print function, in the app as a file that can be printed, e.g. from the Files app.

In the app, the file is first placed in the app's cache and handed to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next – a cloud service, a printer or other people – is entirely your decision, and the privacy terms of the service you choose then apply. The provider has no access.

8. PIN lock

The optional PIN lock is a privacy screen, not encryption. Your data remains unencrypted in local storage. Only a salted check value of the PIN is stored. There is deliberately no recovery path, because a back door would defeat the purpose.

9. Diagnostic log

A local log of technical messages helps with troubleshooting. It stays on your device, can be viewed and cleared in the settings, and is never transmitted. If you want to send it with a bug report, you have to copy and send it yourself.

12. In-app purchase and ratings

Unlock. Without a purchase, the app can be used for one school year with up to two terms, with every feature. Unlimited school years are unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase. Payment and processing are handled exclusively by the respective store and its account. The app only asks the store whether the unlock has been purchased and remembers the result on the device. No identifier of our own is created or transmitted, and no grade data is sent to the store. We receive no names or payment data, only aggregated sales reports from the store.

Ratings. After some time of use, the app asks for a rating at most once per version, using the operating system's rating dialog. Whether and when the dialog appears is decided by the operating system. You submit a rating directly to the store; the app does not learn whether or how you rated it.

13. Children

The app is explicitly intended for students under 16 as well. As no personal data is collected, transmitted or evaluated, parental consent under Art. 8 GDPR is not required. There are no ads, no subscriptions, no chat and no social network integration. The only purchase is the one-time unlock through the store (section 12), where the store's parental controls apply (e.g. Apple's “Ask to Buy”).

14. Your rights

You have the rights to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Because the provider holds no data about you, you exercise them directly in the app: “Export backup” gives you everything in machine-readable form, every entry is editable, and “Reset everything” deletes all of it. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
